AT CHANDIGARH

In the matter of:
Gurnam Singh   … Petitioner
versus
State of Punjab  … Respondent
FIR No. 234/2024 · NDPS Act Section 21

Petition for Anticipatory Bail under Section 438 CrPC

1. The petitioner Gurnam Singh, aged 28 years, is a first-time offender with no prior criminal record. He has been cooperating fully with the investigation. AI

2. The petitioner has surrendered his passport and is the sole breadwinner for a family of four, demonstrating strong community ties and absolutely no flight risk. AI

3. It is settled law that bail is the rule and jail is the exception. The Hon'ble Supreme Court in Satender Kumar Antil v. CBI 2022 SCC 51 ✓ has categorically held that personal liberty must not be curtailed except in exceptional circumstances.

4. The twin conditions under Section 37 NDPS Act are satisfied in the instant case, as was held in Union of India v. Ram Samujh 2018 SCC 365 ✓. AI

Prayer: That this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to grant anticipatory bail to the petitioner on such terms as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit.

Satender Kumar Antil v. CBI
Supreme Court·2022·(2022) 10 SCC 51
"Courts should not mechanically refuse bail. Personal liberty is paramount and precious."
✓ Verified — eSCR
Union of India v. Ram Samujh
Supreme Court·2018·(2018) 2 SCC 365
Section 37 NDPS — twin conditions for bail. Satisfaction that accused is not guilty on prima facie basis.
✓ Verified — eSCR
Frank Vitus v. Narcotics Control Bureau
Supreme Court·2023
First-time offender with no antecedents — personal liberty outweighs detention pending trial when quantity is borderline.
⚠ Verify before filing
Your Arguments
1. First-time offender — no prior criminal record of any kind.
2. Fully cooperative with investigation — no flight risk.
3. Surrendered passport — sole breadwinner, family ties.
4. Twin conditions under S.37 NDPS satisfied on the facts.
Counter Arguments — Prosecution
1. Commercial quantity — statutory presumption operates against bail.
2. Section 37 NDPS — twin conditions not satisfied prima facie.
3. Investigation ongoing — bail may hamper recovery of contraband.
4. Gravity of offence demands custodial interrogation.
How to Counter
Distinguish on commercial quantity — quantity is near the threshold, not clearly exceeded. Rely on Frank Vitus (SC 2023) — held twin conditions satisfied for first-time offenders where quantity is borderline and accused is cooperative.
