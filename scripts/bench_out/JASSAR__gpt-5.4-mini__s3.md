# Executive Brief

## Result

- The appeal filed by **Anubhav Walia** was **dismissed with costs** by the **Additional District Judge, Patiala** in **CA CIS No.216 of 2020** decided on **22.09.2025**.
- The trial court judgment and decree dated **04.03.2020** dismissing the suit were **upheld**.
- Costs were awarded, and counsel’s fee was assessed at **Rs. 2,500/-**.
- No interest direction was made.

## Core Issue

- Whether the appellant had proved, by reliable documentary and oral evidence, that he was entitled to possession and injunctions in respect of the specific suit property allegedly in the unauthorized possession of **Col. R.P.S. Brar**.

## Key Facts

- The appellant claimed rights over **525 square yards** of land at **Stadium Road, Patiala**, described as part of property linked to earlier leasehold arrangements originating from **Dera Garib Dass**.
- He pleaded that the heirs of **Narata Ram** had authority to create further lease rights and that a GPA/lease chain culminated in his entitlement to possession.
- He alleged that **Col. R.P.S. Brar** had forcibly कब्जा/occupied the green-marked portion, constructed buildings, and let out portions to other occupants.
- The respondents disputed the appellant’s title/possession claim and asserted that the disputed area formed part of a different parcel, specifically linked to their own lease chain and revenue record.
- The defence also pleaded that the appellant’s documents did not match the plaint description and that no proper demarcation of the suit property had been conducted.
- The trial court dismissed the suit on **04.03.2020**.
- The appellant challenged that dismissal in this appeal.
- **Respondent No.2** was proceeded against ex parte in the appeal.

## Court's Reasoning

- **Finding:** The appellant failed to prove the suit property with exact dimensions, boundaries, and location.
  **Evidence:** The court compared the plaint, **Ex.P18**, Mark-A, and the revenue/documents relied upon by the appellant and found that the descriptions and directions were inconsistent.
  **Impact:** Without precise identification of the property, the claim for possession and injunction could not succeed.

- **Finding:** The appellant’s core documents did not establish a reliable title/possession chain.
  **Evidence:** The court noted that the GPA/lease documents did not mention clear khasra/khewat/khatauni particulars in a manner that matched the plaint, and the documents were internally contradictory.
  **Impact:** The appellant’s asserted right to possession was treated as unproven.

- **Finding:** No demarcation report supported the appellant’s case.
  **Evidence:** The court specifically relied on the absence of any revenue demarcation and noted that an application for demarcation had been dismissed and not challenged.
  **Impact:** The court was unwilling to hold that the respondent was occupying the appellant’s claimed land.

- **Finding:** The appellant’s oral evidence did not cure the documentary defects.
  **Evidence:** In cross-examination, the appellant could not satisfactorily explain the property description, correction of boundaries, or how the documents matched the disputed site.
  **Impact:** His testimony did not establish illegal possession by the respondent.

- **Finding:** The draftsman evidence was incomplete and unreliable for identification purposes.
  **Evidence:** PW2 admitted he had not entered the suit property for measurement and had not fully accounted for the relevant area on the site plan.
  **Impact:** The site plan could not prove the exact area allegedly कब्जे में.

- **Finding:** The trial court’s dismissal was legally sustainable.
  **Evidence:** On reappraisal of the entire oral and documentary record, the appellate court found no illegality, ambiguity, or perversity in the trial court’s approach.
  **Impact:** The appeal was dismissed and the lower court decree affirmed.

## What This Case Means

- **For Property Litigants:** A possession claim will fail if the property is not identified with precision by matching pleadings, title documents, and site evidence.
- **For Service/Trial Lawyers:** Oral testimony cannot rescue a case where the documentary chain is contradictory and no demarcation is obtained.
- **For Draftsmen/Survey Evidence Users:** A site plan has limited value unless the witness actually measures the relevant land and can explain the basis of the plan.
