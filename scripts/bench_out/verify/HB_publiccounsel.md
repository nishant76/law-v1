# Executive Summary

## Purpose
- Provide a **sample employee handbook** template for an organization (placeholders throughout as **[ORGANIZATION]**, **[TITLE]**, etc.), covering general employment policies, payroll practices, benefits, and certain statutory leaves.
- Expressly warns it **is not legal advice** and **should not be used “as is”**; recommends attorney review and modification for the organization’s actual policies.

## Key Highlights
- **At-will employment**: For employees without a separate fixed-term written contract, employment is **at-will**; can be terminated by either side **at any time, with or without cause or notice**. Change to at-will status only via **written agreement approved by the Board** and signed by a Board Officer.
- **Equal Employment Opportunity / non-discrimination**: Commits to compliance with federal/state/local EEO laws and lists protected categories (race, national origin including language use, sex including pregnancy/breastfeeding, disability, age, etc.). Reports directed to **[TITLE]**; anti-retaliation stated.
- **Anti-harassment policy**: Prohibits harassment of applicants/employees/interns/volunteers, including by non-employees; provides reporting channels (supervisor, **[TITLE]**, President/Executive Director). Investigation process, interim measures, confidentiality to fullest extent possible, and anti-retaliation.
- **DFEH administrative deadline (harassment)**: States complaints to **California DFEH** must be filed within **one year from the date of the alleged unlawful conduct**.
- **Sexual harassment training (conditional bracketed section)**: If employer has **50+ employees**, training deadlines are stated (see “Important Dates”).
- **Open door policy**: Encourages informal raising of job-related concerns with supervisor/management; attempts confidentiality but may share as needed.
- **Personnel files access**: Current/former employees (or authorized representative) must be given access **no later than 30 days** from request; file cannot be removed without authorization; no adding comments; exclusions for confidential items.
- **Timekeeping**: Prohibits “off the clock” work; supervisor must pre-authorize overtime **in writing**; supervisor must initial timesheet changes.
- **Meal/rest breaks**: Provides unpaid meal periods and paid rest breaks for non-exempt employees; prohibits managers from impeding breaks; violations may lead to discipline up to immediate termination.
- **Overtime**: Non-exempt overtime requires **prior written approval** (absent emergency); describes CA-style daily/weekly overtime and double time triggers.
- **Benefits overview**: Official plan documents control over handbook summaries.
- **Vacation**: Regular full-time employees accrue vacation on a tiered biweekly schedule; **90-day waiting period** before use; cap at **twice annual allotment**; vacation paid out on termination.
- **Holidays**: Lists paid holidays; weekend holiday observation requires approval by Executive Director/President; eligibility conditions around working days before/after holiday absent prior approval.
- **Sick leave**: Accrual **1 hour per 30 hours worked**; usage after **90 calendar days**; cap **48 hours or six workdays** (whichever greater); minimum usage increment **two hours**; sick leave generally **not paid out on termination** but reinstated if rehired within **one year**.
- **Jury duty**: With 90 days’ service, paid leave up to **[10] working days per year** (bracketed number); emergency/unplanned DV-related court appearance requires written evidence within **15 days** of absence.
- **CFRA/FMLA leave framework**: Eligibility criteria stated; notice requirements (generally **30 calendar days’ notice** for foreseeable leave); medical certification timing (generally **within 15 calendar days** of request; for foreseeable leaves, **before leave begins**).

## Important Dates
- **Employee access to personnel file**: Must be provided **no later than 30 days** after request is submitted to Human Resources or designated senior management.
- **DFEH harassment complaint deadline**: **1 year** from the date of the alleged unlawful conduct (as stated in the policy).
- **Sexual harassment training (only if bracketed 50+ employee section is adopted)**:
  - All employees: within **first 3 months** of employment; then **at least once every 2 years**.
  - Supervisory/management hires/promotions: **2 hours** interactive training within **first 6 months** of assuming the position; then **at least once every 2 years**.
- **Vacation usage waiting period**: Vacation may be used only after **90 calendar days** from date of hire/eligibility.
- **Sick leave usage eligibility**: Employee must have worked **at least 90 calendar days** before using accrued sick leave.
- **Jury/witness duty documentation (DV emergency/unplanned appearance)**: Written evidence due **within 15 days** of the absence.
- **CFRA/FMLA foreseeable leave notice**: **30 calendar days’ advance notice** required; if not given without reasonable excuse, employer may delay leave until **30 days after notice** is provided.
- **CFRA/FMLA medical certification**:
  - Generally due **within 15 calendar days** after employer’s request.
  - For foreseeable leave: certification must be provided **before leave begins** (when possible).
  - Recertification: due **within 15 calendar days** of request (unless impracticable despite diligent, good-faith efforts).
- **Family care/bonding timing rule**: Family care leave for birth/adoption/foster placement must be **concluded within 1 year** of birth/adoption/placement; and FMLA leave for these purposes must be taken in **consecutive workweeks** and completed within **12 months** following birth/placement.

## Risks / Constraints
- **Template/placeholder risk**: Multiple critical fields are blank or bracketed (e.g., **[ORGANIZATION]**, reporting **[TITLE]**, fiscal year, vacation request lead time **[INSERT NUMBER]**, benefit eligibility start dates, paid jury duty days **[10]**, and optional alternative sick leave scheme in brackets). Using it without tailoring may create inconsistency or noncompliance with actual practices.
- **Policy may create operational obligations**: Several provisions impose strict internal process requirements (e.g., break compliance enforcement, documentation and investigation tracking, written pre-approval for overtime, personnel file access within 30 days).
- **Training obligations are conditional**: The sexual harassment training block applies only if employer has **50+ employees** (as written); adopting/omitting it affects compliance posture.
- **Potential conflicts with plan documents/law**: Handbook says official benefit plan documents control; also multiple leave rules are summaries and may require alignment with current law and the organization’s actual headcount/location facts.
