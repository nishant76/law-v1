# Executive Summary

## Purpose

- Aquaporin A/S’s **Annual Report 2022** presents the company’s business performance, strategy, sustainability agenda, governance, and audited financial statements for **2022**.
- The report is intended to inform shareholders and other stakeholders about the company’s operations, key developments, financial position, and outlook for **2023**.

## Key Highlights

- Aquaporin describes itself as a water technology company based on **Aquaporin Inside®**, a biomimetic membrane technology inspired by aquaporin proteins.
- The company reports **86 employees** on average in **2022** and operations in **Denmark, China, Singapore, Turkey, and the US**.
- Revenue for **2022** was **DKK 30.6 million**, up **243%** versus **2021**.
- The company reports revenue growth across all business areas, led by **Drinking Water** at **DKK 23.0 million**.
- **EBIT before special items** was **(DKK 108.4 million)** and **EBITDA before special items** was **(DKK 89.9 million)**.
- Cash and cash equivalents at year-end **2022** were **DKK 29.4 million**.
- In **February 2023**, the company raised **gross proceeds of DKK 74 million**.
- The report highlights commercial progress including partnerships with **Philips Water Solutions**, **Vestel**, **Forward Water Technologies**, **Flavourtech**, and others.
- The report states that Aquaporin’s technology removes **more than 99%** of PFAS from drinking water, with a cited **Aarhus University** study showing **99.9%** removal.

## Important Dates

- **2022**: Reporting period covered by the annual report.
- **February 2023**: Capital raise of **DKK 74 million**.
- **February 2023**: Launch of the **A2O Pure** water purifier.
- **H1 2023**: Planned launch of the **A2O Kitchen Bar**.
- **2023**: Revenue guidance of **DKK 50–70 million** and EBITDA guidance of **negative DKK 105–95 million** before special items.
- **2024**: Target to increase under-represented gender in the workforce to **45%** and females on the Board to **33%**; also a target to increase females in Management to **33%** by **2024**.

## Risks / Constraints

- The company says the **global economy**, **war in Ukraine**, and **rising inflation** create uncertainty for **2023**.
- It identifies environmental risks linked to **scope 3 emissions**, post-use membrane waste, non-recycled materials, and chemicals used in membrane production.
- It identifies supply-chain risks relating to **human rights**, **corruption**, and **bribery** due to its global operations.
- The report states that further commercialization depends on continued investment, partnerships, and additional financing until operations become cash positive.
- The report notes that used membranes are often **incinerated or landfilled**, and that upcycling/remanufacturing has not yet been implemented.

## Action Items

- Review the **2023 financial guidance** and funding assumptions against the company’s cash position and capital needs.
- Monitor execution of the **A2O product launches** and the commercialization of PFAS-removal solutions.
- Assess contract and partnership implications arising from the company’s expanded collaborations in **Drinking Water**, **Industrial Water**, and **Food & Beverage**.
- Track the company’s stated ESG targets, especially around **diversity**, **emissions**, and **circularity**.
- Follow the planned rollout of industrial water solutions, including landfill wastewater treatment projects in **China**.

## Key Takeaways

- Aquaporin ended **2022** with strong top-line growth but still material losses and negative operating cash flow.
- The company’s near-term commercial thesis is centered on **Drinking Water**, particularly PFAS removal and branded purifier sales.
- Management expects continued growth in **2023**, but the report expressly flags macroeconomic and financing uncertainty.
- Sustainability is positioned as a core part of the company’s strategy, not a separate disclosure exercise.
