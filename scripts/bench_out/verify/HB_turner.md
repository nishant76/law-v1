# Executive Summary

## Purpose
- Sets out **general employment guidelines and workplace policies** for employees of **Turner Industries Group, LLC and affiliated companies** (the **“Company”/“Turner”**), including conduct, ethics, reporting, attendance, anti-harassment/discrimination/retaliation, safety, and drug/alcohol/contraband rules.
- States the handbook **is not an employment contract** and employment is **at-will**, while separately flagging that a **Company Dispute Resolution Agreement (DRA)** is a **separate binding contract** (details of the DRA are not included in the excerpt).

## Key Highlights
- **Applicability / jobsite overlay**
  - Applies to **all Company employees** (salaried/hourly; exempt/non-exempt).
  - For employees at **client/customer/owner jobsites**, handbook **does not replace jobsite-specific policies**; stricter jobsite rules may supersede handbook policies in some areas (expressly noted for attendance).
  - States employees are **not jointly/dually employed** with clients/contractors; **Turner is the sole employer** for handbook purposes.
- **No contract / at-will**
  - Handbook expressly states it **is not a contract**; employment is **at-will** unless a **separate written employment agreement** provides otherwise.
  - Company reserves the right to **modify/revoke/suspend/withdraw** handbook provisions **at any time**.
- **NLRA Section 7 savings clause**
  - Handbook states it is **not intended to unlawfully restrict** employees’ **Section 7 NLRA** rights (protected concerted activity).
- **Ethics / Code of Conduct**
  - Prohibits bribery/illegal payments; requires avoidance of conflicts of interest.
  - **No cash or cash equivalents** may be given or accepted as gifts/entertainment.
  - Good-faith reports of Code violations must be reported to **1-800-626-1735**; retaliation is prohibited.
- **Corporate fraud & whistleblower**
  - Covers suspected fraud/wrongful conduct (including by vendors/contractors).
  - Reporting channels: supervisor/designated administrator or **Hotline: 1-800-626-1735** (anonymous permitted).
  - Reprisal prohibited; retaliation complaints may be made to supervisor/HR/Employee Relations **1-800-288-6503** or Hotline.
  - Investigations: Internal Audit leads, coordinated with Office of General Counsel; only OGC may speak to attorneys/government investigators.
- **Employee conduct / discipline**
  - Discipline can include oral/written reprimands, final warnings, suspension without pay, termination; not necessarily progressive.
  - Extensive list of terminable conduct includes safety violations, falsification, violence/threats, drugs/alcohol/weapons policy violations, insubordination, no-call/no-show, horseplay/bullying, confidentiality breaches, theft, sleeping on duty, and legal violations.
- **Attendance**
  - Absence/tardiness requires notice to supervisor and/or timekeeper “as early as possible prior.”
  - “As a general rule,” jobsite employee absent **2+ consecutive days** without notifying supervisor and timekeeper **may be disciplined up to termination**.
  - Late arrival/early departure requires **prior supervisor approval** (and timekeeper notice if applicable).
- **Anti-harassment / anti-discrimination / anti-retaliation**
  - Prohibits hostile work environment conduct based on protected characteristics; includes improper use of Company communications systems for prohibited content.
  - Provides complaint channels: supervisor/manager, regional HR, Personnel, Employee Relations **1-800-288-6503**, or Hotline **1-800-626-1735**; immediate/as soon as possible reporting required; no need to complain first to the offending supervisor.
  - Company may impose strict confidentiality during investigations; breach can lead to discipline up to immediate termination.
- **ADA**
  - Provides for reasonable accommodation via an “interactive process” initiated by notifying supervisor and contacting **Turner Medical** (and/or Employee Relations); requires medical documentation/clearance to return to work; Company makes final accommodation decision.
- **Separation of employment**
  - Voluntary resignations: requested **written notice at least 10 working days** before last day (holidays and PTO **do not count**); compliance generally results in resignation “in good standing” and rehire eligibility absent extenuating circumstances.
  - Exit meeting may occur to collect property and discuss final pay; COBRA info will be sent to last known address (timing not stated).
- **Health, Safety & Environmental (HSE)**
  - HSE compliance is a **condition of employment**.
  - Unsafe conditions/practices and **any incident/injury** must be reported to supervisor **immediately**; failure may result in discipline up to termination.
  - Badge/inspection checks may be required when entering/leaving buildings/jobsites.
- **Drug, Alcohol & Contraband (DAC)**
  - Prohibits drugs (including **trace amounts**; includes marijuana “to the extent allowed by law,” plus hemp oil and CBD), alcohol, firearms/weapons/explosives/ammunition, stolen property, unauthorized prescription drugs, drug paraphernalia on Company or jobsite premises.
  - Prohibits **any detectable amount of alcohol** at work; prohibits reporting to work with **any detectable quantity** of prohibited drugs in system (subject to applicable law/regulation).
  - Allows searches/inspections (including dogs) to the maximum extent allowed by law.
  - Drug testing may occur post-offer/pre-hire; random; reasonable suspicion; post-accident; return-to-work; fit-for-duty; client-required, etc.
  - Refusal to test / failure to show / refusal to provide specimen (subject to exceptions) may result in discipline up to termination or withdrawal of offer.
  - Positive tests: initial positive confirmed by certified lab and MRO review; upon second confirmed positive, offer withdrawn or employment terminated.
  - Right to discuss confirmed positive results with Turner Medical; ADA interactive discussion contemplated.
  - State-law conflict clause: “WHERE STATE LAWS CONFLICT WITH THIS POLICY, THE APPLICABLE STATE LAW WILL APPLY.”
- **IT / email / messaging**
  - Network transmissions (including email) are Company property; Company may monitor/access at its discretion; employees should not expect privacy.
  - Prohibits sending proprietary/trade secret/confidential info except via Company secure platform.
  - Employees must report receipt of unlawful/inappropriate material to Hotline **1-800-626-1735**.
  - Company auto-deletes email on a Company-established timeline (timeline not stated); records subject to holds must be archived/moved to exempt locations.
  - Password sharing is prohibited.

## Risks / Constraints
- **At-will employment** and explicit **no-contract** language limits employee reliance on handbook provisions as contractual promises.
- **Jobsite policies may supersede** handbook attendance-related rules (and potentially other stricter site rules), creating compliance complexity for site-assigned employees.
- **Broad monitoring/no-privacy** position for IT/email may create employment and data-handling exposure if not administered consistently with applicable law (the excerpt repeatedly conditions monitoring/testing/searches “to the maximum extent allowed by law”).
- **DAC Policy** includes marijuana/CBD/hemp prohibitions “to the extent allowed by law” plus a state-law conflict clause, requiring careful **state-by-state** alignment.
- **Strict confidentiality** expectations in investigations: breach may lead to discipline up to immediate termination (as stated), which can be sensitive in practice.

## Key Takeaways
- This is a **policy handbook**, not an employment contract; employment is **at-will** unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.
- Multiple policies impose **mandatory reporting** (ethics/fraud/harassment/HSE) using a centralized **Hotline (1-800-626-1735)**, with **anti-retaliation** commitments.
- The handbook anticipates significant **jobsite-specific rule overlay**, especially for attendance and client-mandated DAC requirements.
