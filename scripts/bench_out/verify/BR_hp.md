# Executive Summary

## Purpose

- This document is a product data sheet for the **HP Smart Tank Plus 550 All-in-One Printer Series**.
- It markets the printer’s main features, technical specifications, supported supplies, accessories, and service options.
- It also includes warranty, connectivity, environmental, and regional contact information.

## Key Highlights

- The printer supports **print, copy, scan, and wireless** functions.
- It is positioned as a **high-capacity ink tank system** with **low cost per page** and **up to 2 years of ink included in the box**.
- It offers **dual-band Wi‑Fi**, **HP Smart app** mobile printing, and direct printing features.
- Claimed print performance includes up to **22 ppm** black draft, **16 ppm** colour draft, **11 ppm** black ISO, and **5 ppm** colour ISO.
- It supports borderless printing up to **210 x 297 mm** and manual duplex printing.
- The product page includes ordering details for **HP Smart Tank Plus 551** and **555** variants, replacement bottles, HP Care Packs, and recommended papers.

## Important Dates

- The document is dated **May 2019**.
- Warranty coverage stated in the document: **one year or 30,000 pages**, whichever is earlier, from the **date of purchase**.
- No other fixed deadlines are specified.

## Risks / Constraints

- Wireless compatibility notes vary across the document: one note says compatibility with **2.4 GHz and 5.0 GHz**, while another note states wireless operations are compatible with **2.4 GHz routers only** and excludes wireless direct.
- Actual print yield is stated to vary depending on usage patterns, content, settings, temperature, humidity, and whether printing is infrequent or in one colour.
- Some ink from the included bottles is used to start up the printer.
- Not all listed supplies are available for sale in all countries.
- Power-voltage conversion is warned against; the document says converting operating voltages will damage the printer and void the warranty.

## Action Items

- Confirm the relevant regional specification, especially the wireless compatibility note, before relying on the connectivity claim.
- Check whether the **HP Smart app** download is required for the intended mobile-printing workflow.
- Verify local availability of the listed replacement bottles, papers, and Care Packs.
- Review the warranty period against the purchase date: **one year or 30,000 pages, whichever is earlier**.
- If planning high-volume use, assess whether the stated **recommended monthly page volume of 400 to 800 pages** fits the intended workload.

## Key Takeaways

- The document is a marketing and technical specification sheet, not a legal or judicial document.
- The main commercial message is that the printer combines **wireless connectivity, mobile printing, and high-volume low-cost ink-tank printing**.
- The most practically important constraints are the **wireless note inconsistency**, the **variable yield caveat**, and the **limited warranty/service terms**.
