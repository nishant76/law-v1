# Executive Summary

## Purpose
- Provide **Urban Design Partners, PLLC (UDP)** workplace policies and employee expectations, including EEO/anti-harassment, operational rules (hours, hybrid work, timekeeping), benefits, leave, and standards of conduct (discipline up to discharge, at-will language).

## Key Highlights
- Equal Employment Opportunity: prohibits discrimination on listed protected characteristics; provides for disability and religious accommodation (unless undue hardship); complaint escalation to supervisor and then HR contact **anna@adepthrmsolutions.com** if no satisfactory response within **5 business days**.
- Non-harassment and Sexual Harassment: prohibits harassment; reporting pathway mirrors EEO (supervisor → management/HR) with the same **5 business day** escalation trigger; confidentiality “to the extent possible”; anti-retaliation; cooperation in investigations required; violations can lead to discipline up to discharge.
- Drug-Free and Alcohol-Free Workplace: prohibits unlawful/unauthorized use/possession/sale/distribution of controlled substances, paraphernalia, or alcohol on premises or on company business; prohibits working under the influence (with narrow prescription exception); expressly states no right to work under the influence of medical marijuana; exceptions possible for “responsible drinking” at business meetings/social outings; discipline up to discharge.
- Workplace Violence: zero tolerance for threats/aggression/violence (includes stalking/cyberstalking, brandishing weapons, intimidation); immediate reporting to any management member; prompt investigation; anti-retaliation; corrective action may include notifying authorities.
- Employee classifications:
  - Full-time: **30+ hours/week** (not short-term).
  - Part-time: **<30 hours/week** (not short-term).
  - Short-term: project/freelance/per diem/temp; generally not eligible for company benefits.
  - Exempt: salaried, exempt duties, paid at least **$684/week ($35,568/year)**; no overtime.
  - Non-exempt: overtime-eligible; overtime for **>40 hours/week** at **1.5x**.
- Working hours: business hours **8:30 am–5:30 pm, Mon–Fri**; “core hours” **8:30–3:30** even on modified/flex schedules.
- Hybrid work: not an entitlement; generally “in office most of their time,” remote up to **2 days/week** if approved; must be in-office minimum **3 days/week** set by studio manager; eligibility includes job suitability, performance/work habits, and general guideline of **6 months employment** and **1 year experience** (case-by-case).
- Hybrid request: must email studio manager specifying schedule; submit at least **14 days** before requested effective date; meeting is then set to review criteria.
- Timekeeping: non-exempt must record actual start/end and departures; falsification prohibited (discipline up to discharge). Exempt must record daily attendance and full-day absences.
- Overtime: non-exempt only; requires prior supervisor authorization; workweek defined as **Sunday 12:00 am to Saturday 11:59 pm**.
- Pay: paid **bi-monthly**; direct deposit required/used.
- Safe harbor (exempt): lists permissible salary deductions (e.g., full-day absences, disciplinary suspensions, FMLA, offsets for jury/military pay, first/last week partial, full week no work); prohibits salary reductions for partial-day absences in weeks where any work is performed (with leave-bank deductions possible where allowed); improper deductions should be reported immediately to a supervisor (or another supervisor if inappropriate/unavailable).
- Benefits (high-level; subject to plan documents/SPDs; UDP can modify/terminate):
  - Paid holidays for full-time employees include specified days; office closure period **Dec 24–Dec 31** with office closing **5:30 pm Dec 23** and reopening **8:30 am Jan 2** (with weekend adjustment rules stated).
  - Open Time Off (OTO) for “professional staff” full-time employees; no accrual/no payout on termination; scheduled time off requires advance supervisor approval; generally not approved for more than **2 consecutive weeks** (exceptions reviewed by Partners); employee must notify colleagues at least **2 weeks** before planned time off.
  - After **5 consecutive days** unscheduled time off or **10 days** unscheduled within any **30-consecutive-day** period, additional unscheduled days may be treated as Personal Leave (per policy).
  - Paid maternity: eligible full-time employees with **6 consecutive months** prior to birth; **8 consecutive weeks** paid (first **4 weeks 100%**, next **4 weeks 60%**); requires formal leave of absence request.
  - Paid paternity: full-time with **6 months** continuous service at birth; **2 weeks 100%** plus up to **2 additional weeks at 50%**.
  - Adoption/legal guardian: **$2,500 stipend** paid upon completion with proof; paid leave for eligible employees (same structure as paternity).
  - Workers’ compensation: immediate reporting; incident report per **North Carolina Industrial Commission**; may involve drug/alcohol testing by insurer; formal leave of absence required if missing work.
  - Bereavement (full-time): immediate family up to **5 paid days**; extended family up to **3 paid days**; death of spouse or child up to **10 paid days**; must be on regularly scheduled consecutive workdays following day of death; verification may be required.
  - Domestic violence leave (NC): reasonable unpaid leave; notice as soon as practical unless imminent danger; certification may be required; employee may request OTO.
  - Voting leave: where required by state law; should notify supervisor at least **2 days** prior “where possible.”
  - Wellness reimbursement: full-time eligible; up to **$50/month** with receipt submitted each month to Office Administrator; reimbursed monthly; taxable unless rules say otherwise.
  - Employee referral award: **$500 gross** for regular full-time hires if referred candidate is hired, completes **3 months** service, and referrer still employed (excluding managers in line and HR personnel).
  - 401(k): eligible upon hire; employer match up to **6%** of income.
- Standards of conduct: lists examples of unacceptable conduct; at-will employment stated; progressive discipline “endeavor” but discretion to terminate at any time for any reason.
- Punctuality/attendance: notify supervisor as early as possible, no later than start of workday; must call every day of absence; unreported absences of **3 consecutive workdays** “generally” treated as voluntary resignation.
- Communications/computer systems: no expectation of privacy; UDP may monitor/access; passwords/private access codes must be provided to management upon request; violations can lead to discipline up to discharge.
- Social media: no posting during work time or using company equipment; do not disclose proprietary/client info (including client name); disclaimer required if mentioning UDP and expressing political opinion or opinion on UDP’s actions that could create conflict of interest; violations can result in discipline up to discharge.
- Portable communication devices: company devices primarily for business; monitoring possible; using company-issued PCD for personal text messages prohibited at all times; if personal device used for business, employee must submit device to IT for reset on/before last day (may delete personal data); texting/e-mailing while driving prohibited in all circumstances.
- Inspections: UDP may require inspection of persons, possessions, vehicles on company/client property, and work areas; employees must cooperate.
- Smoking: prohibited on premises and in company vehicles.
- Solicitation/distribution: prohibited during work time; non-employee solicitation/distribution prohibited at all times; restrictions on employee distribution in work areas/working time.
- Confidential information: misuse/disclosure can lead to discipline up to termination; employees may be required to sign an agreement reiterating obligations.
- Record retention: employees must contact the President regarding potential/actual litigation, audits, investigations affecting retention; failure can lead to civil/criminal sanctions and discipline up to discharge.

## Important Dates
- Handbook date: **August 2023**.
- Paid holiday closure rules include: office closed **from 5:30 pm on December 23** through **December 31**, reopening **8:30 am on January 2** (with weekend adjustments described).
- Performance review cadence (depending on position/classification): **30 days**, **90 days**, **6 months**, then **annually**.

## Risks / Constraints
- At-will/discipline: multiple policies expressly allow discipline “up to and including discharge” and reserve discretion on discipline/termination.
- Monitoring/no privacy: broad monitoring/access rights over email/voicemail/internet and portable devices; personal data loss risk on IT reset for personal devices used for business.
- Hybrid work: explicitly not an entitlement; manager can revoke “at any time for any reason,” with written notice including end date.
- OTO constraints: time off requests can be denied/cancelled/rescheduled for business needs; generally not approved beyond two consecutive weeks; no payout of unused time on termination.
- Reporting windows: EEO/harassment policies require escalation if no satisfactory response within **5 business days**; failure to follow reporting procedures may affect outcomes (e.g., workers’ comp procedures affecting benefits).
- Attendance: **3 consecutive workdays** of unreported absence “generally” treated as voluntary resignation.

- For EEO/harassment complaints: report immediately to supervisor; if no satisfactory response within **5 business days**, escalate to management/HR at **anna@adepthrmsolutions.com**.
- To view personnel file: submit written request to **Brian Smith** or HR (**anna@adepthrmsolutions.com**) and schedule review with UDP representative.
- For wellness reimbursement: submit a detailed receipt monthly to the Office Administrator to claim up to **$50/month**.
- For personal leave: submit written request to supervisor at least **2 weeks** before start; provide medical certification if medical; notify expected return date at least **1 week** before leave ends.

## Key Takeaways
- The handbook is a policy baseline emphasizing anti-discrimination/anti-harassment enforcement, strict safety and conduct rules, and managerial discretion (including at-will termination and revocable hybrid arrangements).
- Several employee-facing timelines are explicit and operationally important: **5 business days** escalation for EEO/harassment response, **14 days** advance for hybrid requests, **2 weeks** advance for personal leave requests and colleague notice for planned OTO, and **3 consecutive unreported workdays** risking deemed resignation.
