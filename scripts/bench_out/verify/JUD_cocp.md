> **Winning Argument:** The respondents showed that the required approvals and a **Punjab Governor’s notification dated 16.12.2016** had already renamed **Sunam railway station** as **“Sunam Udham Singh Wala Railway Station”**, fully redressing the petitioner’s grievance.

> **Key Takeaway:** A petition seeking mandamus becomes infructuous and is liable to be disposed of when the sought relief is implemented during pendency, leaving no surviving cause for further directions.

# Executive Brief

## Result
- The contempt petition / mandamus-related proceeding (**COCP-1484-2016**) was **disposed of as infructuous** because the railway station renaming sought by the petitioner had already been approved/implemented.
- No costs or interest: **Not specified in the order.**

## Core Issue
- Whether any further court direction was required to rename **Sunam railway station** to **“Sunam Udham Singh Wala Railway Station”** after the petitioner’s earlier representation-direction and subsequent governmental action.

## Key Facts
- **H.C. Arora** (petitioner, appearing in person) sought a direction “in the nature of mandamus” to change the name of **Sunam railway station** to **Sunam Udham Singh Wala railway station**.
- The petitioner asserted the town had already been named **“Sunam Udham Singh Wala”**, so the railway station name should match.
- The petitioner had earlier filed a writ petition in which the Court directed that his **representation be considered by the appropriate authority** (case details not specified in the order).
- Union of India’s counsel submitted that **necessary approval** for change of name had been granted.
- The State counsel produced a **notification dated 16.12.2016** issued by the **Governor of Punjab** renaming the existing Sunam railway station (District **Sangrur**) as **“Sunam Udham Singh Wala Railway Station”**.
- State counsel also referred to a **communication dated 24.06.2016** to the **Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India** (content summarized in the order as referencing Shaheed Udham Singh’s sacrifice).

## Court's Reasoning
- **Finding:** The petitioner’s grievance stood redressed because the station’s name had already been changed to **“Sunam Udham Singh Wala Railway Station.”**  
  **Evidence:** Statement by UOI counsel that approval had been granted, and the **Punjab Governor’s notification dated 16.12.2016** produced in Court (taken on record).  
  **Impact:** No surviving controversy; petition required no further adjudication.

- **Finding:** No further direction was necessary.  
  **Evidence:** The Court noted the station name now matched the town’s name and accepted the governmental steps as “in order.”  
  **Impact:** The petition was **rendered infructuous** and disposed of.

## What This Case Means
- **For mandamus / public-interest style litigants:** If the government implements the requested administrative action during the case, the Court is likely to dispose the matter as **infructuous** rather than issue further directions.
