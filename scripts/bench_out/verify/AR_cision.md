# Executive Summary

## Purpose
- This is **Investor AB (publ)**’s **Annual Report 2022**, presenting the group’s business model, strategy, sustainability priorities, governance, and financial performance for **2022**.
- The report is a translation of the official Swedish annual report published in ESEF format and is stated to be a **non-official version**.
- It covers Investor’s three business areas: **Listed Companies**, **Patricia Industries**, and **Investments in EQT**.

## Key Highlights
- Investor states its ultimate objective is to generate an attractive total shareholder return, with an annual return requirement of **8–9%**.
- For **2022**, total shareholder return was **-15%**, compared with **-23%** for the **SIXRX return index**.
- Adjusted net asset value was **SEK 673bn** at year-end **2022**, with growth of **-10%** including dividend added back.
- Market capitalization was **SEK 584bn**.
- Proposed dividend for **2022** is **SEK 4.40 per share**, a **10%** increase from **SEK 4.00**.
- Investor reports leverage of **1.5%** at year-end, with no debt maturities until **2029**.
- Net cash flow from **Investments in EQT** reached a record **SEK 6.2bn**.
- Investor says **100%** of portfolio companies had targets aligned with the **Paris Agreement** by the end of **2022**.

## Important Dates
- **1916**: Investor was founded by the Wallenberg family.
- **1917**: The Wallenberg Foundations began awarding grants funded by portfolio-company dividends.
- **2022**: Reporting year covered by the annual report.
- **October 2022**: **Accelleron** was spun off from **ABB**.
- **May 2023**: Proposed dividend installment of **SEK 3.30 per share**.
- **November 2023**: Proposed dividend installment of **SEK 1.10 per share**.
- **2029**: Earliest stated debt maturity.

## Risks / Constraints
- The report identifies a challenging environment marked by **war, increased protectionism, rapid technological change, climate change, inflation, higher interest rates, energy price shocks, and supply-chain disruption**.
- Investor highlights geopolitical and geoeconomic risks as now being a regular board-level concern.
- The report cautions that investors and companies can no longer rely on global supply chains to the same extent as before.
- It states the report is a **translation** and **non-official version** of the Swedish annual report.
- No express legal deadlines or compliance dates are created in the document beyond the proposed dividend timing.

## Key Takeaways
- Investor’s 2022 performance was weak in market-return terms but still outperformed the broader Swedish market.
- The group’s strategy remains centered on **engaged ownership**, disciplined capital allocation, and long-term value creation.
- Sustainability is presented as an integrated strategic priority rather than a standalone initiative.
- The report signals continued confidence in the balance sheet and dividend capacity.
