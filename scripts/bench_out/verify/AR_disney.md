# Executive Summary

## Purpose
- Annual disclosure on **Form 10-K** filed with the **U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission** covering **The Walt Disney Company and subsidiaries** for the **fiscal year ended 01.10.2022**.

## Key Highlights
- Corporate profile: **The Walt Disney Company** is a diversified worldwide entertainment company operating in two segments: **Disney Media and Entertainment Distribution (DMED)** and **Disney Parks, Experiences and Products (DPEP)**.
- Listing/registered security: **Common Stock ($0.01 par value), trading symbol DIS**, listed on the **New York Stock Exchange**.
- Key corporate identifiers:
  - **Commission File No. 001-38842**
  - **Delaware** corporation; **EIN 83-0940635**
  - Principal offices: **500 South Buena Vista Street, Burbank, California 91521**; tel. **(818) 560-1000**
- Reporting status / compliance checkboxes (as stated in the document):
  - **Large accelerated filer**
  - Filed all required Exchange Act reports in prior 12 months: **Yes**
  - Submitted Interactive Data Files in prior 12 months: **Yes**
  - Filed auditor attestation under **Sarbanes-Oxley Act Section 404(b)**: **Yes**
  - Shell company: **No**
- Equity/market data (as disclosed):
  - Aggregate market value held by non-affiliates (as of last business day of the most recent second fiscal quarter): **$249.5 billion**
  - Shares outstanding (as of **16.11.2022**): **1,823,591,988**
- COVID-19 disclosure: COVID-19 and mitigation measures materially affected operations, particularly **DPEP** (park closures/suspended cruises/tours) and **DMED** (delayed/shortened/cancelled theatrical releases; production disruptions); impacts continued due to safety measures, restrictions, closures in some international markets, and production disruptions.
- Human capital disclosure:
  - Employees as of **01.10.2022**: approximately **220,000** (approx. **166,000** U.S.; **54,000** international)
  - Workforce composition: approx. **78% full time**, **15% part time**, **7% seasonal**
  - Significant collective bargaining coverage in parts of the business; some non-U.S. employees represented by works councils/trade unions/associations.
- Environmental/sustainability goals (2030):
  - Achieve **net zero Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions** for direct operations by **2030**
  - Achieve **zero waste to landfill** at wholly owned and operated parks and resorts by **2030**
- Notable business plans/forward-looking items explicitly stated:
  - **Ad-supported Disney+** planned for the U.S. in **December 2022**; international rollout starting **late 2023**
  - DPEP pipeline includes openings/expansions and cruise ship deliveries (e.g., Disney Cruise Line ships expected in **fiscal 2025**, **fiscal 2026**, plus a separately purchased partially completed ship expected delivered in **2025**; Lighthouse Point destination scheduled to open **2024**)
  - Hulu governance: Disney has **67% ownership** and operational control; **put/call** with NBCU exercisable beginning **January 2024**

## Risks / Constraints
- Forward-looking statements caveat: the report expressly states forward-looking statements are based on assumptions as of the report date and are subject to known/unknown risks and uncertainties (including economic conditions, competition for content, consumer preferences, regulatory/political/military developments, technology, labor markets, adverse weather/natural disasters, and content availability), with COVID-19 amplifying risks.
