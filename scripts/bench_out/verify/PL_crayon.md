# Executive Summary

## Purpose
- Provide a **tiered “Product Launch Playbook”** that defines recommended **deliverables** and approximate **timelines** based on the level of launch:
  - **Tier 1 (External): New Product/Service (~6 weeks)**
  - **Tier 2 (External): New Feature (~5 weeks)**
  - **Tier 3 (External): Updated Feature (~2 weeks)**
  - **Tier 4 (Internal): Internal Update (~2 weeks)**

## Key Highlights
- The playbook uses a matrix showing which launch activities are:
  - Required (**✔**),
  - Not required (**✘**), or
  - Conditional (**If Applicable**) depending on the launch tier.

- Items included across multiple external tiers:
  - **Product Brief** (Tier 1–2 required; Tier 3–4 “If Applicable”)
  - **In-Product (UI) Announcement** (Tier 1–3 required; Tier 4 not required)
  - **Inclusion in Customer Newsletter** (Tier 1–3 required; Tier 4 “If Applicable”)
  - **Dedicated customer communication (Email or in-app)** (Tier 1–3 required; Tier 4 not required)
  - **Updated Website** (Tier 1–3 required; Tier 4 “If Applicable”)
  - **Sales Enablement/Demo Assets (Script, video, etc.)** (Tier 1–3 required; Tier 4 not required)
  - **New/Updated Sales Training** (Tier 1–3 required; Tier 4 not required)
  - **New/Updated Pitch Deck** and **Data Sheet** (Tier 1–3 required; Tier 4 not required)

- Tier-specific items:
  - Tier 1 requires additional brand/launch infrastructure items including **Trademark Registration** and **Domain Registration** (both **✔** only for Tier 1).
  - Tier 1 includes PR-heavy elements:
    - **Press Release with Customer Quote** (✔ Tier 1; “If Applicable” Tier 2; ✘ Tier 3–4)
    - **Press/Analyst Outreach** (✔ Tier 1; “If Applicable” Tier 2; ✘ Tier 3–4)
  - **Demand Gen Outreach/Program** is required for **Tier 1 and Tier 2**; not required for Tier 3–4.
  - **Paid Media (SEO, Banner)** is required for **Tier 1 and Tier 2**; not required for Tier 3–4.
  - **Pricing Updates** are required for **Tier 1**; “If Applicable” for Tier 2; not required for Tier 3–4.
  - **New/Updated Deal Qualification Process** is required for **Tier 1** only.

## Important Dates
- No calendar dates are specified in the document.
- Only approximate launch durations are provided:
  - **Tier 1:** ~6 weeks
  - **Tier 2:** ~5 weeks
  - **Tier 3:** ~2 weeks
  - **Tier 4:** ~2 weeks

## Risks / Constraints
- Many deliverables are marked **“If Applicable”** without criteria; applicability standards are **not specified in the document**, which can create ambiguity in scope and expectations.
- The playbook provides **approximate** timelines (“~”), not firm deadlines; no dependency mapping or sequencing is stated.

## Action Items
- Determine the intended launch level (**Tier 1–4**) based on whether it is a **new product/service**, **new feature**, **updated feature**, or **internal update**.
- Using the matrix, compile a launch checklist identifying:
  - Mandatory deliverables (**✔**),
  - Excluded items (**✘**), and
  - Items to be assessed for applicability (**If Applicable**).
- For **Tier 1**, plan for **Trademark Registration** and **Domain Registration** as required deliverables (the document does not specify jurisdiction, filing process, or timing beyond inclusion in the tier).

## Key Takeaways
- The document is a **scoping tool**: it standardizes launch workstreams by tier and indicates which cross-functional deliverables are expected at each level.
- Only **Tier 1** explicitly includes brand/protection steps (**trademark** and **domain**), implying greater legal/brand coordination for a full external product launch.
- No enforceable deadlines, owners, approval gates, or governance steps are provided in the document.
