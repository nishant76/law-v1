# Executive Summary

## Purpose
- This is a product brochure / launch document for the **2026 Camry**.
- It markets the vehicle’s design, performance, technology, safety, connected services, warranties, trims, and disclosure information.

## Key Highlights
- The **2026 Camry** is presented as an all-hybrid model with up to **51 combined EPA-estimated mpg** and up to **232 net combined hp** in AWD configuration.
- Available **Electronic On-Demand All-Wheel Drive (AWD)** is described as automatically sending torque to the rear wheels when front-wheel slippage is detected.
- The brochure highlights multiple drive modes: **Normal**, **Sport**, **Eco**, and **EV**.
- Trim lines shown include **LE**, **SE**, **Nightshade**, **XLE**, and **XSE**, with different feature sets and packages.
- The document emphasizes Toyota Safety Sense™ **3.0**, blind-spot monitoring, rear cross-traffic alert, traffic jam assist, and other driver-assist features.
- Connected services include **Remote Connect**, **Safety Connect**, **Service Connect**, **Drive Connect**, and **Wi‑Fi Connect**.
- Warranty coverage and ToyotaCare are summarized, including hybrid battery coverage and no-cost maintenance / roadside assistance.
- The brochure also lists exterior/interior color and wheel options, plus package availability.

## Important Dates
- **2026** model year for the Camry.
- **1-year** Remote Connect trial is included on select trims.
- **5-year minimum** trial subscription is included for **Safety Connect**.
- **5-year minimum** trial subscription is included for **Service Connect**.
- **2 years or 25,000 miles**: ToyotaCare scheduled maintenance coverage.
- **2 years, unlimited mileage**: ToyotaCare roadside assistance.
- **3 years or 36,000 miles**: basic warranty.
- **8 years or 100,000 miles**: hybrid-related component warranty.
- **10 years from date of first use or 150,000 miles**: hybrid battery warranty.

## Risks / Constraints
- Many features are described as **available** rather than standard, so actual equipment depends on trim and packages.
- Several connected services and safety features are **4G network dependent** and may require a trial or subscription.
- The brochure states that some services and features require **app registration**, acceptance of terms, and may involve **data charges**.
- The document says information is based on data available at the time of posting and is **subject to change without notice**.
- Certain features have operational limits and should not be relied on exclusively, as noted in the disclosures.

## Action Items
- Confirm the desired **trim level** before relying on any specific feature set.
- Check whether a feature is **standard or optional** on the chosen grade.
- Review connected-services eligibility, trial periods, and subscription requirements.
- Verify compatibility of wireless charging, Apple CarPlay®, Android Auto™, and device-dependent features.
- Review the warranty and ToyotaCare terms in the applicable warranty/maintenance guide before purchase.

## Key Takeaways
- The brochure positions the **2026 Camry** as a hybrid sedan focused on efficiency, connectivity, and driver-assist technology.
- Key commercial differentiators are AWD availability, hybrid power output, advanced infotainment, and bundled service trials.
- Actual vehicle content may vary significantly by trim, package, and market availability.
