# Executive Summary

## Purpose
- To describe **“The Digital Launch Playbook”** (a spreadsheet-based product by **Joel Zaslofsky / Value of Simple**) and explain what it includes, why it exists, and how a purchaser should use/customize it for digital launches.

## Key Highlights
- The Playbook is positioned as a **single spreadsheet** combining: a checklist, task sequence, best practices, and a “game plan” for **pre-launch, launch, and post-launch** activities for digital projects.
- It contains **over 200 tasks** with fields such as: task name/description; required/optional/N/A; categories and tags; people/teams involved; systems/processes involved; who to notify on completion; and customizable start/end timing relative to launch (“number of days to start/end before product launch”).
- It includes curated **best practices** and **hyperlinks** to publicly available source material.
- The product package includes:
  - The **spreadsheet** (with tabs including **Quick Start Guide**, **Instructions–Legend**, and the main Playbook tab)
  - A “how to” video
  - A “real example” setup/modification video
  - This overview document
- Compatibility/usage constraint: recommended use is **Microsoft Excel 2000 or above**; the document states some functionality/benefits **cannot be harnessed** in other spreadsheet programs (examples given: **Google Docs** and **OpenOffice**).
- Sharing/licensing restriction (express statement): the Playbook **“can only be used by someone who purchased it”**; sending a copy to others is stated as **“Not legally.”**
- Support limitation: the creator states **no support beyond the purchase or refund process**.
- Affiliate program: stated **Yes** (no further details provided).

## Risks / Constraints
- **License/use restriction:** The document states the Playbook **cannot legally be shared** with a friend/family/co-worker/contractor/etc.; it “can only be used by someone who purchased it.”
- **Software constraint:** Full functionality is tied to **Microsoft Excel 2000+**; other spreadsheet tools may not support all functionality/benefits.
- **Support limitation:** No support is offered **beyond purchase/refund** (as per the FAQ).
- **Printing:** Document notes the spreadsheet is **not formatted for printing** or easy physical use (printing is optional but not optimized).

## Action Items
- Confirm internal compliance with the stated restriction that the Playbook **may only be used by the purchaser** and **should not be forwarded** to third parties.
- If implementing, plan to use **Microsoft Excel 2000 or above** to access the stated features/benefits.
- Follow the recommended initial setup sequence in the document (watch the two videos; read the **Instructions–Legend** tab; copy the template; filter/sort categories; mark tasks optional/required/N/A; customize task ownership/systems/timing/tags; optionally customize columns and add/delete tasks; optionally save as a reusable template).

## Key Takeaways
- This document is an overview of a **spreadsheet-based launch planning product**, not a legal instrument; the only explicit legal/compliance-relevant statement is the asserted **no-sharing / purchaser-only use** rule.
- There are **practical constraints** around tool choice (**Excel 2000+**) and expectations (**no support beyond purchase/refund**).
