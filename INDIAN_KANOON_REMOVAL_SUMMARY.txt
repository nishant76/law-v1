# Indian Kanoon Removal Summary

**Date**: April 8, 2026  
**Reason**: Indian Kanoon is now a direct competitor (launched Prism AI)  
**Status**: ✅ COMPLETE — All Indian Kanoon references removed from codebase

## Executive Summary

All Indian Kanoon scraping code, imports, and documentation have been completely removed from the Nikhar codebase in a single operation. The codebase now contains zero references to Indian Kanoon as a data source.

**Key fact**: The Citation model and database migrations were never tightly coupled to Indian Kanoon — they use generic `official_source` and `source_url` fields that work with any government source (eSCR, P&H HC, district courts). This makes Phase 2 extension to other sources seamless.
